# ETHOnline — Magic Internet Frens submission video

**Target runtime:** 3:25 (inside the 2–4 min window)
**Voice:** unhurried, warm, a little wry. Retro-futurist — a legend told about a machine,
not a pitch about a protocol.
**Look:** hand-drawn pixel art against clean chrome. CRT scanlines, phosphor glow, chunky
monospace lower-thirds. Warm, not cold sci-fi.

**Music:** open on the soft flute/strings cue — the acoustic warmth against the retro-future
visuals is the whole joke, and it sells "old magic, new machine". At 1:50 (perps) let a low
synth arpeggio slide in underneath and carry the back half. Lift at 3:00 for the rebirth.

> ⚠️ *Concerning Hobbits* is Content-ID'd — a public YouTube upload will likely get claimed
> or muted. Use a royalty-free equivalent for the public cut (prompt: "pastoral Celtic flute,
> warm strings, 90bpm" → then "analog synth arpeggio, Tangerine Dream, warm tape"), and keep
> the real cue for the private judging link if you want it.

### On the fee line (read before recording)

The VO says fees go to the **genesis dividend** and to **buying back the token that backs the
NFT floors**, and that's accurate as stated. The precise mechanics, if a judge asks:

- The guild slice goes to the genesis dividend — that part is direct.
- The buyback slice market-buys the live token to back **the live iteration collection's**
  floor. Iteration #2 is the exception: it continues the MiFrens collection, so its buyback
  is **split** between the forged frens' ledger and the genesis reserve — both floors rise
  together there.
- The **genesis** floor otherwise ratchets from its own loop, not the buyback: redeeming
  sends a fren to the treasury, anyone can buy it back at 2× floor, and that payment plus the
  re-enchant fee go into the reserve — so the floor only moves up.

Saying "buys back the floor under the NFTs" covers all of it without over-claiming that every
iteration's fees buy the genesis floor directly. Sources: `public/magicfrens-llm.md`
§5.2, §7.1, §8, §8.1.
