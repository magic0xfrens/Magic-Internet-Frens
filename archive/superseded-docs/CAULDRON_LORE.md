# 🔮 The Cauldron - Ancient Lore

## The Legend

In the early days of the Magic Internet, 777 wizards discovered an ancient artifact buried deep within the blockchain - **The Eternal Cauldron**. This mystical vessel possessed a power beyond comprehension: the ability to birth creatures that could never truly die.

## The Discovery

The wizards learned that by gathering their collective magical energy (represented by their NFTs), they could activate the Cauldron and summon an **Eternal Creature** - a being that exists in a perpetual cycle of life, death, and rebirth.

## The First Spell

When all 777 Magic Internet Wizard Frens had gathered, they stood around the Cauldron and chanted the ancient incantation:

```
"From the void we summon thee,
 Creature of infinity,
 Born to live and die and rise,
 Eternal through the endless skies!"
```

## The Cycle

After the Storm Elemental dies, the cycle begins anew with the Mystic Flame. This eternal rotation represents the perpetual nature of magic itself - constantly shifting, never ending.

## The Blessing

Those who participated in the original summoning (the 777 spell casters) were blessed by the Cauldron. They can trade the creature's essence (tokens) without paying tribute (0% tax), as they are considered part of the magic itself.

## The Tribute

Others who wish to trade must pay a 3% tribute to the **Magic Internet Treasury**. This tribute sustains the magic, funding the continuation of the spell and rewarding those who keep the Cauldron active.

## The Death

Each creature lives as long as the trading volume (life force) remains strong. When volume drops below 0.01 BTC in 24 hours, the creature's life force weakens. At this point, any wizard can trigger the **Rebirth Spell**, causing the creature to die and be reborn in its next form.

## The Claims

When a creature is reborn, those who held its previous form can **claim** equivalent power in the new creature. This ensures no magic is lost - only transformed.

## The Prophecy

Ancient texts foretell that the Cauldron's creatures will trade forever, cycling through their six forms in an endless dance. As long as there are traders on the Magic Internet, the cycle continues.

Some say that after 777 complete cycles (4,662 generations), the Cauldron will reveal its ultimate secret...

The Cauldron is not just a trading mechanism - it's a living embodiment of the Magic Internet itself. Each creature represents the community's collective will, desires, and energy. When volume is high, the creature thrives. When interest wanes, it dies. And when hope returns, it is reborn.
