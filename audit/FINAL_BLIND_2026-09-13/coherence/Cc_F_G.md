### Economic sense — the incentive that breaks first
**Nobody is paid to move the floor.** The creature floor is funded by the legacy buffer (swap fees) plus royalties, which only become a floor when someone calls the permissionless, unrewarded `materializeLegacyReserve()` (`CauldronRegistry.sol:1354-1356`) — and, for ERC20 royalties, the equally unrewarded `RoyaltyRouter.sweep` (`:101`), whose address is published nowhere (FG-2). Both are pure gas costs with a diffuse benefit; the default equilibrium is that neither runs.

**On death, genesis is paid first and creatures absorb the shortfall.** `relaunch()` folds `genesisPending` into `genesisReserveOutstanding` and reserves it in full (`CauldronRegistry.sol:970-971`), then subtracts the *aggregate* `collectionLedger.totalEntitled()` clamped to zero with a `ReserveShortfall` event (spec `G_floors_redemption.md:320-324`). OG floor is made whole; creature floor is silently truncated.

**Claims are first-come, not pro-rata.** `redeemOgFren` reverts `NoBalance` when the reserve is short (`RedemptionExt.sol:99`) and `recycleCollection` reverts `"reserve short"` (`PoolOps.sol:1373`) rather than paying a partial. On a shared, under-covered reserve that makes redemption a gas race the last holder always loses — and Invariant R is enforced only at payout, never at credit (`G_floors_redemption.md:270-292`, still accurate).

## Verdict

The F/G *contracts* are coherent with their own spec: the multi-asset basket, the
per-generation ledger floor, the 2× ratchet and the live-token denomination all do what
sections F and G say, and today's RoyaltyRouter change genuinely closes the ERC20-royalty
strand at the contract layer. The incoherence is one layer up and it is systematic: **every
non-native rail the contracts grew is invisible to the product.** The dividend UI claims only
ether, the indexer decodes only the ether events, the churn call is hard-coded native, the
RoyaltyRouter has no address in any config and no caller for its keeper-free `sweep`, and
`07-FEES.md` still documents a royalty receiver that reverts. Nothing here loses a user's
money — accruals survive transfers as `owedAsset`, the ERC20 churn path fails safe with a
revert, and the vault's inertness is honestly documented in two of three places. But a
protocol whose whole design thesis is quote rotation ships a front end and an indexer that
only work while the quote is ether. Fix FG-1 (add the basket to `useMiFrensDividend` and
`DividendAbi`) before any rotation, then FG-2 and FG-4.
