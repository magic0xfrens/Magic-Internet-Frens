## Verdict (≤200 words)

The decimals class that dominated the last coherence pass is **mostly** closed — swap, stake and the
dividend basket all carry the quote's real decimals now. It survives in exactly one place, and it is
a place nobody re-checked because it predates the rotation feature: the collection-floor buyback
threshold is a hardcoded `0.02 ether` compared against a buffer denominated in the live quote
(B-4), mirrored by the indexer's `/1e18` (B-5). On a USDG generation the floor's engine stops and
the UI keeps promising it is running.

The larger coherence problem this run is not units at all — it is **three statements the shipped
bundle makes that the chain does not honour**: a one-hour gacha settle window that is 25.6 seconds
(B-1), an "anti-whale cap" set to the entire supply (B-2), and "forged frens don't vote" on a
contract that is `ERC721Votes` over every id with no genesis filter (B-3). All three were confirmed
by executing against the built bundle, the live deployment, or a passing PoC — not by reading
`src/`. B-3 is executed end to end: 64 self-liquidations minted 64 badges and **64 votes**,
uncapped, at 0.0757 ETH each, while the UI tells users the electorate is 1111 genesis frens.

**The single most likely way a user loses money or trust through a mismatch rather than an exploit:**
a player reads "settle them within the hour", walks away, and his crystal expires in twenty-six
seconds — he paid real credit, got nothing, and the UI told him he had time.
